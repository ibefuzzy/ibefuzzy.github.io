What it actually does

Droid Tycoon has 5 rebirth cycles, 35 levels each, 3 droids per level, each needing a specific rarity. That's a lot to hold in your head. This tracks it for you.

Log a droid once — by hand, or automatically by reading your screen — and every overlay updates instantly: what you still need, what's safe to retire, and how long until the next timed event.

Everything the overlay does

All of it optional, all of it toggle-able with its own hotkey.

🎯

Current Rebirth Requirements HUD

An always-on-top panel showing exactly which droids and rarities you still need for your upcoming levels.

🧬

Full Rebirth Requirements overlay

A bigger checklist view of everything left in the whole active cycle, not just the next few levels.

♻️

Safe to Retire list

Shows Legendary/Mythic droids you've already logged that this cycle will never ask for again.

⏱

Event timers

Live countdowns to Stellar, Mythic and Galactic blueprint drops, plus the rotating mission timer. Pure clock math — no screen reading involved.

📸

Auto-logging via OCR

Manually trigger a read of your Rebirth screen or Crafting Bench and it fills in what you own — you confirm before anything saves.

⌨️

Hotkeys for everything

Every overlay, every trigger, fully rebindable — plus a one-press "hide all" for clean screenshots and a live on-screen hotkey list.

Will this get me banned?

This overlay only ever does three things: reads pixels off your own screen (for the optional OCR buttons), reads your system clock (for the timers), and reads/writes a local file on your own computer (to remember what you've logged). It never touches Fortnite's process, memory, or input in any way — it's just an ordinary always-on-top window sitting over the game, the same category as a stopwatch app or OBS.

No process injectionNever reads or writes Fortnite's memory.
No input simulationNever sends clicks or keystrokes into the game.
Screen + clock onlyEverything it "sees" is optional, manual, and on-screen only.
