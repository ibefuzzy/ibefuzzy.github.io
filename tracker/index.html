📖 How to use this

  1. If you're charting your rebirth cycles — which droid unlocks at which level — switch to the By Rebirth Level view in the toolbar below.

  2. You'll get a layout like this — one row per rebirth level, three droids per row:

    Tap or click a droid to log it at that exact rarity — tap it again to clear it. If a tap doesn't seem to do anything, you've already logged a higher rarity for that droid somewhere else. Either way, press and hold (or right-click on desktop) any of its cells to open a menu listing every rarity — tap the checked one to clear it, or tap a different one to set that as what you own.

Good to know
  • The A–Z view works the same way — tap a droid's rarity dot to log it, tap your current best again to undo it, or press and hold (right-click) a dot for that same set-exact-rarity menu.
  • A SELL tag next to a name means that's the last time this droid is needed, at any level or cycle — safe to fuse or sell once you own it.
  • A dimmed name in the By Rebirth Level view is already covered by a higher rarity you logged elsewhere — nothing to do there.
  • 🧬 Rebirth Reqs lists everything the selected cycle asks for, low to high, in one panel.
  • ♻ Safe to Retire lists droids you own that the cycle is done asking for, grouped by tier (tap a tier to hide/show it) — enter your current rebirth level in that panel first.
  • On a phone, tap the already-lit A–Z or By Rebirth Level button again to hide the droid list, so 🧬 Rebirth Reqs / ♻ Safe to Retire sit right under the toolbar instead of at the bottom of a long scroll. Tap either one to bring the list back.
  • ⏱ Event Timers shows live countdowns to the next Stellar/Mythic/Galactic drop and the next mission.
  • Export a backup before clearing site data, switching browsers, or moving to a new device, then Import it back. Reset all clears every droid you've logged and can't be undone.
